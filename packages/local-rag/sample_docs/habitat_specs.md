# Orion Project: Habitat Architecture

The habitat for the Orion project is based on the **Modular Inflatable Design (MID)**.

## Structural Features
- **Shielding**: Multi-layer thermal insulation and radiation shielding made of high-density polyethylene.
- **Airflow**: Independent life support systems for each module.
- **Power**: 100kW solar arrays coupled with hydrogen fuel cells for night operations.

## Interior Layout
1. **Module A**: Living quarters and kitchen.
2. **Module B**: Hydroponics lab for food production.
3. **Module C**: Mission control and communication hub.
4. **Module D**: Exercise and medical facility.

The air pressure inside the habitat is maintained at **700 mbar** with a gas mix of 21% Oxygen and 79% Nitrogen.
