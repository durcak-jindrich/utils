# Orion Project Overview

The Orion Project is a next-generation space exploration initiative aimed at establishing a sustainable human presence on Mars.

## Key Objectives
1. **Mars Colony Phase 1**: Establish a modular habitat for 12 astronauts.
2. **Resource Harvesting**: Extract water from subsurface ice and oxygen from the Martian atmosphere.
3. **Connectivity**: Deploy a satellite constellation for high-speed communication between Earth and Mars.

## Timeline
- **2028**: Launch of the precursor robot mission.
- **2030**: Arrival of the first crewed mission.
- **2035**: Full-scale industrial operations begin.
